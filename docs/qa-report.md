# PATH: QA report, phases 1 to 3

17 November 2026. Tested: the clickable demo (`prototype/`) as on branch `qa-sim`, which is `main` plus the Explore navigation change (PR #15). Nothing in the app was changed for this report.

## How it was tested

- **Simulation harness** (`pnpm sim`, 63 scripted checks): six personas and the stress cases from the brief, run against the same engine the screens use, then read back from every screen where the result must show. 31 pass, 16 are confirmed defects (marked with their QA number, so the run stays green and a marker must be removed when the defect is fixed), 16 describe things that are not built yet. The other eight findings below come from the crawler and the direct probes and have no marker yet.
- **Crawler** (`pnpm qa:crawl`): every role, tab and club, and every button one click deep. 687 screen states and 653 clicks per width, at 360, 390 and 430 px. It reports script errors, dead buttons, sideways overflow, cut-off text, small tap targets, controls without a name, and accessibility violations (axe, WCAG A and AA).
- **Existing suite** (`pnpm test:proto`, 69 tests): booking, waitlist, recurring holds, credits, coach chat, Explore, log, review, privacy. All pass.
- **Direct probes** for time (midnight, both daylight-saving switches), script injection, keyboard use, reload, offline, speed and layout shift.

All of it runs with fixed time (Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00, Europe/Zurich unless the test moves the clock).

### Assumptions and limits

- **One athlete can be played** (Mia Keller, a member of both clubs). The scripts written for "Jonas" and "Lea" run on her account.
- **The demo data was not rebuilt to the brief's size before reporting.** It has 48 members at PAU (the admin's Members list shows 13), two named coaches at PAU and five at the studio, one open invoice and no overdue one, PT packs only as a product line. Building 120 members, overdue invoices and PT packs means building features, so it is listed under "not built" and proposed for phase 4.
- **"30 people on the last spot at once" cannot be tested.** The demo is one person's phone with no server. The engine-level version passes (30 attempts, one booking, one debit). The real test belongs to the backend, which is not built.
- **The live site was not tested from here** (pathpau.netlify.app and the model behind "Build with PATH"); the test machine cannot reach it.

## The ten UX improvements with the most impact

1. **One plan, one truth.** Merge the athlete's own plan, the coach's shared plan and the AI's changes into the one "Your plan" on Path. An eased week, an approved change and a booking all show there, on the day they belong to. (QA-13)
2. **Booking that never surprises.** A cancel hands the spot to the next person waiting; a clash is named before the tap; a session the club cancels returns the credit and tells every booked member; an expired pass says so and offers the renewal. (QA-01 to QA-04)
3. **Back works.** The phone's Back closes a sheet, then goes one screen back. It is the most used gesture on a phone and today it leaves the app. (QA-10)
4. **A first five minutes.** Onboarding that asks for the club, the goal and what to share, so the Path is the member's own from the start. Today the goal cannot even be changed. (N-01)
5. **A coach chat that understands people.** Put the model behind the chat, still acting only through confirm cards, so "make this week easy" works in any wording and no reply is a dead end. Alarming symptoms get a clear "stop and get help". (QA-11, QA-12)
6. **Each club a closed room, each list the same people.** Scope a coach's notes, plans and groups to their club, and use one member list per club for the coach, the sessions and the admin. (QA-06, QA-07, QA-19)
7. **Tell me what just happened.** After every booking and cancel: what changed on the pass, where to find it, and an Undo for a cancel. Keep the focus where the person was. (QA-09)
8. **Empty states that lead somewhere.** "No bests yet. Log a timed run and it appears here", with the button. (QA-17)
9. **A pass you can read at a glance.** What is left, what it covers, when it ends, what happens at the month's end, and a nudge before the last session is used. (QA-04)
10. **Admin Home that answers "how is my club doing?"** One sentence, the numbers against last month, and "who hasn't paid" one tap away. It is the screen investors will look at longest. (N-04)
