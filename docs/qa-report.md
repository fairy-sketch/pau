# PATH: QA report

17 November 2026. Phases 1 to 3 tested the clickable demo (`prototype/`) as on branch `qa-sim` and changed nothing. Phase 4 (branch `qa-fix`) fixed all 24 findings and built the demo data. Steps 0 to 6 (branch `build-n`) fixed what Ramon's own test found and built the not-built items N-01 to N-04, the season's end and "Start demo". This file is the state after step 6; the original findings and repro steps are kept in the table.

## How it was tested

- **Simulation harness** (`pnpm sim`, 86 scripted checks): six personas and the stress cases from the brief, run against the same engine the screens use, then read back from every screen where the result must show. 85 pass; one is left for the backend (thirty real members on the last spot at the same moment).
- **Crawler** (`pnpm qa:crawl`): every role, tab and club, and every button one click deep. 790 screen states and 756 clicks per width, at 360, 390 and 430 px, 0 findings. It reports script errors, dead buttons, sideways overflow, cut-off text, small tap targets, controls without a name, and accessibility violations (axe, WCAG A and AA).
- **Whole demo suite** (`pnpm test:proto`, 154 passing with the simulation, 1 left for the backend): booking, waitlist, recurring holds, credits, coach chat, Explore, log, review, privacy, admin, coach, onboarding, PT, the tour.
- **Direct probes** for time (midnight, both daylight-saving switches), script injection, keyboard use, reload, offline, speed and layout shift.

All of it runs with fixed time (Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00, Europe/Zurich unless the test moves the clock).

### Assumptions and limits

- **One athlete can be played** (Mia Keller, a member of both clubs). The scripts written for "Jonas" and "Lea" run on her account.
- **The demo data** was rebuilt in phase 4: 120 invented members and four coaches per club, open, overdue and paid invoices, PT packs in use, a 10-session pack at PAU.
- **"30 people on the last spot at once" cannot be tested.** The demo is one person's phone with no server. The engine-level version passes (30 attempts, one booking, one debit). The real test belongs to the backend, which is not built.
- **The live site** (pathpau.netlify.app) was read in a browser and compared with the prototype; see "Live site against the prototype". The model behind "Build with PATH" was not called, because every call spends API credit.

## Still open

- **The other members are simulated.** A held spot is always taken by the person it is held for; thirty real people on one spot cannot be tested without a backend.
- **The coach chat is still a script.** It reads more wordings and some German and answers in English. A model behind it, acting only through confirm cards, is the real fix.
- **Nothing is sent for real.** Messages to members stop in the demo's outbox; a PT pack or a reminder moves no money. Sending and payment belong to the backend.
- **Club health, the briefing and the drafts are written by fixed rules**, not by a model. The only model in the build is behind "Build with PATH" on the live site.
- **Only PAU has personal training**; Studio Demo's coach has none.
- **The live site is behind the prototype** until PR #17 and `build-n` are merged (next section).
- **Tested in Chromium only.** Safari's and Firefox's engines could not be installed on the test machine.

## The ten UX improvements with the most impact (from phase 3)

1. **One plan, one truth.** Merge the athlete's own plan, the coach's shared plan and the AI's changes into the one "Your plan" on Path. An eased week, an approved change and a booking all show there, on the day they belong to. (QA-13)
2. **Booking that never surprises.** A cancel hands the spot to the next person waiting; a clash is named before the tap; a session the club cancels returns the credit and tells every booked member; an expired pass says so and offers the renewal. (QA-01 to QA-04)
3. **Back works.** The phone's Back closes a sheet, then goes one screen back. It is the most used gesture on a phone and today it leaves the app. (QA-10)
4. **A first five minutes.** Onboarding that asks for the club, the goal and what to share, so the Path is the member's own from the start. Today the goal cannot even be changed. (N-01)
5. **A coach chat that understands people.** Put the model behind the chat, still acting only through confirm cards, so "make this week easy" works in any wording and no reply is a dead end. Alarming symptoms get a clear "stop and get help". (QA-11, QA-12)
6. **Each club a closed room, each list the same people.** Scope a coach's notes, plans and groups to their club, and use one member list per club for the coach, the sessions and the admin. (QA-06, QA-07, QA-19)
7. **Tell me what just happened.** After every booking and cancel: what changed on the pass, where to find it, and an Undo for a cancel. Keep the focus where the person was. (QA-09)
8. **Empty states that lead somewhere.** "No bests yet. Log a timed run and it appears here", with the button. (QA-17)
9. **A pass you can read at a glance.** What is left, what it covers, when it ends, what happens at the month's end, and a nudge before the last session is used. (QA-04)
10. **Admin Home that answers "how is my club doing?"** One sentence, the numbers against last month, and "who hasn't paid" one tap away. It is the screen investors will look at longest. (N-04)

Numbers 1, 2, 3 and 6 were done in phase 4; 4, 7, 8 and 10 in steps 0 to 3. Open: 5 (a model behind the chat) and 9 (a pass you can read at a glance, beyond the end date).

## Three things to build next to beat Kenko and Eversports

Both are booking and payment tools for the club. Neither knows what the member is training for. PATH's edge is that the booking, the plan and the coach are one thing. (This is a general picture of the two products; they were not looked up for this report.)

1. **The model behind the coach chat, on the one plan.** "Move my long run to Saturday and book me into track instead" as one confirm card that changes the plan and the bookings together. The rules, the confirm cards and the single plan are in place; this is the step from a script to the product.
2. **The coach's day in five minutes** (dashboard B: live session, per-athlete note at check-in, group recap, edit and bulk approve in Review). A booking tool tells a coach who is coming. PATH tells them who needs what today and drafts the follow-up.
3. **The admin's "how is my club doing?"** (dashboard C and D: members at risk with a drafted message, overdue reminders, cancel a session, club health against last month). Retention that comes from the training data, not only from who stopped booking.

State after step 6: numbers 2 and 3 are built in the clickable demo, with fixed rules where a model will later write the drafts. Number 1 is open, and so is the backend all three need to be real (accounts, sending, payment).
