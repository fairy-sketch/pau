Our own icon set

Thick, round, friendly line icons drawn for this app. Use the switches to compare a regular and a bold line, and straight icons against a slight tilt like on your reference sheet.

At work in the app

How they are drawn

  • 24 px gridEvery icon sits in a 24 by 24 box with about 3 px of air around it.
  • One lineA single stroke weight with round ends and round corners. No fills, except small dots.
  • One colourIcons take the colour of the text next to them. They are never lime.
  • Simple shapesEach icon is two or three strokes, so it still reads at 20 px in the tab bar.
